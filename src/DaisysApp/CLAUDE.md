- **3.12.4 (asked 2026-10-26: Daisy's App crashing now and then on the rig, GAMER11; logs ZIP from its export, extracted to `%TEMP%\crashlogs-1225104110`, timeline script `%TEMP%\crashtool\tl.py`):** 46 crash records in 30 days: mostly 0xc0000374 (heap corruption, ntdll) and AccessViolations in unrelated places (MiniMirror `CaptureLoop`, Nvml, ForzaWheel `GameControllers.List`, WPF UIA), several at exit: the signs of memory corruption elsewhere. Nearly all came seconds to minutes after the rig's Xbox 360 pad (045E:028E) on port `9&DBBB1C1&0&4` dropped and 1A34:F517 appeared there, with Button2Key re-listing controllers; today's at the instant Button2Key switched on for Cyberpunk. Cause: `Joy2Key/XInput.cs` called `XInputGetStateEx` (#100), which writes XINPUT_STATE_EX (20 bytes, `dwPaddingReserved` after the gamepad, as in SDL/Wine), into the 16-byte XINPUT_STATE: 4 bytes over the caller's stack on every read of a connected pad (since 0.16). Fix: `dwPaddingReserved` in the struct. Also `GameControllers.Reader` Read/Dispose under `lock(this)` with `disposed` (Button2Key's engine and tab share readers; one disposed outside the lock while the other read). Not verified at run time: no Xbox pad on this PC (probe returned 1167 on all slots). The crashes before 0.16 (MiniMirror `DuplicateOutput1`, 10-06/07) were other bugs, fixed long ago.
